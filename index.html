What gets automated

Let the system handle the predictable steps.

Automation works best when the rule is clear. We map the approved process, build the sequence and test the exceptions before it goes live.

01 · CAPTURE

New enquiries

Create or update the contact, record the source and make sure the right person knows it arrived.

02 · FOLLOW-UP

Consistent next steps

Send approved confirmations and reminders, then flag the conversations that need a person.

03 · OPERATIONS

Tasks and handovers

Create tasks, request missing information and notify the team when work moves to a new stage.

04 · RETENTION

After the sale

Continue approved updates, completion follow-up, review requests and future reactivation.

How AI helps

Use AI where understanding is useful—not where a simple rule will do.

AI can help interpret, summarise or communicate within an approved process. Important decisions and exceptions stay with your people.

RULE

Automation handles predictable actions

Update records, create tasks, send reminders and move work through defined stages.

AI

AI helps understand the context

Identify an enquiry type, collect missing details, answer approved questions or summarise a long conversation.

YOU

People handle judgment

Pricing, advice, approvals, exceptions, relationships and the work that genuinely needs experience.

AI assistance, with boundaries.

We do not position AI as a replacement for your company or promise that it will make every decision correctly.

  • Built around approved business information
  • Tested with clear fallback paths
  • Human handoff when the request needs judgment
  • Adjusted as your real workflow changes
