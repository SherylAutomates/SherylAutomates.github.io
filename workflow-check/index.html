Workflow Health Score

Fair — with a few gaps costing real time

Intake and quoting are working, but follow-up and project handoffs rely heavily on memory. This is the most common gap seen in solo and small service businesses.

1Summary

Enquiries arrive mainly through social DMs and phone, with client details noted informally. Quotes are built manually for each client, and once a project starts, progress is tracked by memory rather than a shared system. The biggest risk sits between "lead goes quiet" and "project is underway" — this is where things are most likely to be forgotten.

2Main Gaps Identified

Lead follow-up has no system
Enquiries that don't reply immediately are followed up "when remembered," not on a schedule.
Likely effect: warm leads go cold before a second contact happens.
Client information lives in one place: memory
No shared record of who a client is, what was discussed, or what stage they're at.
Likely effect: details get repeated or lost, especially under time pressure.
Quotes are rebuilt from scratch each time
No reusable template, so quoting takes longer than it needs to.
Likely effect: slower turnaround, inconsistent pricing presentation.
Project status isn't visible outside your head
Updates to clients happen reactively, when they ask.
Likely effect: more "just checking in" messages than necessary.
