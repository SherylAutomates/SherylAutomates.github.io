We map the gaps before building the system.

The system can

  • Bring connected customer information into one workspace
  • Show owners and staff what needs attention
  • Support normal business WhatsApp text communication
  • Reduce repetitive chasing and manual handovers

The system will not

  • Replace professional judgement or client relationships
  • Guarantee sales or business results
  • Approve pricing or financial decisions by itself
  • Automate a process before the rules are agreed
